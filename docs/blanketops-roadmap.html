Platform Roadmap

BlanketOps

What if a platform didn't just deploy your software — but understood your organization? BlanketOps is a 10-year bet that sovereignty starts at the contract, not the cloud console. Unified orchestration across identity, tenancy, networking, governance, and compute — not as bolted-on integrations, but as a single reconciliation surface. Every resource is declared. Every boundary is intentional. Every layer — from org topology to node substrate — converges toward one thing: deterministic software delivery at any scale, on any infrastructure, on any edge, with or without internet, under your terms. Environments is the door. The rest is the house.

Architecture Principles
Reconciliation
Thin controllers, reconciliation logic in dedicated services. Desired state drives everything.
Contracts
Protobuf-first. Buf codegen. CR shapes derived from contracts, not the other way around.
Auth Model
No admission webhooks. SubjectAccessReviews + AuthFlags capability matrix at the org level.
Networking
Deterministic multi-network substrate. Cilium + Multus, WireGuard mesh, Firezone gateways. From VLAN to overlay.
Multi-Cloud
Org-level cloud provider registry. OCM for multi-cluster orchestration. vClusters for isolation. Resource placement rules for data residency.
Sovereignty
From protobuf contracts to bare metal. gokrazy node images, custom Go runtime, zero external dependencies. The goal is full stack ownership.
